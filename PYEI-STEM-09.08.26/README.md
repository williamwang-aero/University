# Physics & Astrophysics

## Satellites - UNIVERSITY OF AUCKLAND

Auckland University gives a vast amount of oppurtunities to build satellites like CUBSATS. The UNIVERSITY OF AUCKLAND has a lot of conferences and links to many facilities worldwide, including NASA, MIT, CALTECH and more.

A Cubesat is a small square-like satellite which contains sensors and computer inside to gather information in outer space. It can be built by high school students with the right materials and launched into space.
